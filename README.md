# Mini-project-1 
🏦 Banking Performance Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses bank customer data across France, Germany and Spain to understand customer activity, product usage, balances and loyalty, and to highlight where the bank can re-engage customers.

📌 Project Overview

Banks need a clear view of who their customers are, how active they are and which products they use. This project turns raw customer data into a visual dashboard that answers questions such as:

Which country has the most active members?
How many customers hold a credit card?
How many products does a typical customer use?
How long do customers stay with the bank?
Which customer groups hold the highest balances?
🛠️ Tools Used
Tool	Purpose

🔍 Key Insights

France has the highest number of active bank members compared to Germany and Spain.
There are over 10,000 card holders, showing that credit cards are the bank's most successful product.
The average customer salary is over 100K, which points to a wealthy customer group.
Most customers use only 1 or 2 products; very few use 3 or 4.
On average, customers stay with the bank for about 5 years, showing good long-term loyalty.
Only 50% of members are active, so there is a big opportunity to re-engage the inactive 5,000 members.
Male customers in France hold the highest account balances, while female engagement in Spain and Germany is slightly lower.

💡 Recommendations
Run re-engagement campaigns for the inactive 5,000 members.
Cross-sell additional products (savings, loans, insurance) to customers who hold only 1 or 2 products.
Build targeted offers for female customers in Spain and Germany.
Reward long-tenure customers to protect loyalty.
